# Demonstration academic regulations

FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY: These sample rules are for this project's demonstration only.
They are not official rules of any college.

Students must complete all listed prerequisite courses before a new course registration
is accepted. A student may have only one registration record for a given course.
A course marked completed has been finished; enrolled means currently enrolled; pending
means planned or to be completed. Pending does not by itself mean an application awaiting
approval. All three statuses represent existing registration records and must not be
duplicated. A completed course cannot be registered again through this assistant.

The sample minimum attendance is 75 percent per course. A leave projection adds the
classes scheduled on the requested date to the denominator, while attended classes stay
unchanged. It assumes all other attendance totals remain unchanged. Demo timetables are
fictional; holidays and canceled classes are not modeled.
