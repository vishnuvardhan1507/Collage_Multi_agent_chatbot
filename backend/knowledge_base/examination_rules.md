# Demonstration examination rules

FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY: These are sample rules, not official college regulations.

In this demonstration, examination eligibility normally requires at least 75 percent
attendance in each course. Approved leave does not automatically grant eligibility;
an advisor or examination office must review exceptions.

Attendance guidance from the assistant is not formal examination clearance. The assistant
can report recorded attendance and explain this policy, but it does not issue admit cards,
grant examination clearance, or automatically approve exceptions. Formal eligibility and
exceptions require institutional review through the advisor or examination office.

Students should bring a college ID and examination admit card, arrive 30 minutes early,
and leave phones and unauthorized notes outside the examination room. Suspected
misconduct is referred to the examination office. Results displayed by the assistant
are sample academic records, not official transcripts.
