# Student Handbook

The college help desk operates from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days in the Admin Block.
Students should carry their ID card on campus and during examinations.

Minimum attendance for regular theory and lab courses is 75 percent. Students below the
minimum may be asked to meet their advisor and submit supporting documents for approved
absence cases.

Internal assessment marks are shared by course faculty through the academic portal. Final
grades are released by the examination cell after moderation.

Students can contact their faculty advisor for academic planning, prerequisite questions,
course-load guidance, and leave-related support.
