# Student Handbook

The college help desk operates from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days in the Admin Block.
Students should carry their ID card on campus and during examinations.

Minimum attendance for regular theory and lab courses is 75 percent. Students below the
minimum may be asked to meet their advisor and submit supporting documents for approved
absence cases.

Internal assessment marks are shared by course faculty through the academic portal. Final
grades are released by the examination cell after moderation.

Students can contact their faculty advisor for academic planning, prerequisite questions,
course-load guidance, and leave-related support.

## Account access and academic support

Students can view their own academic records, ask about courses and prerequisites, and
register for eligible courses after completing prerequisites. Faculty can view student
academic information only within their own department and review that department's
pending leave requests. Advisor or teaching assignments do not grant access to students
in another department. Only faculty can book classrooms.

Both roles can browse public course, faculty, classroom, hostel, mess, and transport
information. Classroom occupancy does not expose another faculty member's identity or
booking purpose. The chatbot cannot change attendance or perform administrative writes.

## Student leave requests

Provide a start date, end date, and reason. Dates must be ordered and cannot start in
the past. A request fails if its dates overlap an existing pending or approved leave
request for the same student, including shared boundary dates.

Chat leave submission first displays a summary with Confirm and Cancel. No leave request
is submitted until Confirm is clicked and submission succeeds. Cancel creates no request.
Proposals expire after 15 minutes; a newer action proposal in the same conversation
replaces the previous pending proposal. Ordinary chat replies do not confirm an action.
The existing leave-panel Submit button submits directly without this extra chat step.

Students can delete only their own leave requests whose end date is today or later,
including requests ending today. Requests that ended before today cannot be deleted.
The leave panel asks for Yes/No confirmation before deletion. Faculty reviewing pending
requests must supply a reason when rejecting them; students can see that rejection reason.
Dates such as today and tomorrow use the college timezone, Asia/Kolkata.

## Classroom recommendations and booking

Only faculty can book classrooms. Supply a room ID, booking date, start and end times,
and purpose. Include the number of attendees and any AC, projector, or computer needs.
Match all explicit requirements; unknown equipment must not be treated as available.
Required seating means at least the requested number of seats. A request for computers
means at least one, or at least the specified count. If one computer per attendee is
needed, supply the attendance count.

Use the database for classroom inventory, buildings, capacity, equipment, and availability.
Recommendations do not reserve rooms: faculty select a recommended room or explicitly
request a room by ID. Availability requires a database check for the requested date and
time. Without a time slot, list suitable rooms without claiming they are available.
Past booking dates and overlapping bookings are not allowed; adjacent slots are allowed.

Chat shows the room, date, times, purpose, and requirements with Confirm and Cancel.
Nothing is booked until Confirm is clicked and the booking succeeds. Confirm rechecks
permissions, requirements, and conflicts. Cancel creates no booking. Proposals expire
after 15 minutes; a newer action proposal in the conversation replaces the previous
pending proposal. Typing confirmation does not replace clicking Confirm. Other faculty
members' identities and booking purposes remain private.

## Hostel and mess enquiries

Students can check hostel vacancies by sharing type and AC preference, and ask for room
fees and separate mess fees. Students can select a vacant room and book one bed through chat.
Current inventory and prices must be checked in the database.

Room and mess fees are separate charges per student, per month. Either mess plan can
accompany any available room category. Show the charges separately and calculate a
combined monthly amount only when requested. Get available sharing types, AC options,
room counts, occupied beds, and fees from current database records.

Vacant beds equal sharing capacity minus occupied beds. Match all requested preferences
and exclude full rooms when listing vacancies. Recorded vacancies do not guarantee future
availability. A selected room displays a final Confirm/Cancel card with its sharing type,
AC status and monthly room fee. Nothing is allocated until Confirm succeeds; pending cards
do not hold beds and expire after 15 minutes. Availability and prices are checked again
at confirmation. Changed room details require a new confirmation.

Each student may have one active hostel booking. The allocation starts immediately; no
stay dates, payments or mess enrollment are included. Students may ask about their own
booking or cancel it through a final Confirm/Cancel card, releasing their bed after commit.
Faculty can browse hostel availability but cannot book beds. Other students' bookings are private.

Both Veg and Non-veg menus are available to browse for Monday through Sunday, with
breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner listed separately. Ask for a whole day,
week, or a specific meal, such as Tuesday veg dinner. Follow-ups can change the day,
meal, or plan. General menu requests show separately labeled Veg and Non-veg menus.
Missing menu information must be reported as unavailable rather than inventing dishes.
Browsing a menu does not enroll a student in a mess plan or change account information.

## Transport enquiries

The campus is in HITEC City. Students can ask which bus serves their locality and request
pickup points, drop-off points, timings, and fares. Routes, stops, locality aliases, schedules, operating days, and fares are maintained
in the database and are not duplicated here. Suggest only recorded routes and stops; for
an unlisted locality, ask for a nearby listed locality rather than inventing a stop. Transport enquiries do not
reserve seats, process payments, or provide live bus tracking.
