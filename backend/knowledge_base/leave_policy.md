# Leave Policy

Students should submit leave requests before the absence whenever possible. A request must
include the from date, to date, and a clear reason.

Medical leave longer than two working days should include supporting documentation when the
student returns to campus. Event participation leave should be routed through the faculty
coordinator or department office.

Leave requests are first reviewed by the student's advisor or the faculty member connected
to the student's active course. Approved leave may be considered when attendance shortage
cases are reviewed, but it does not automatically change attendance records.
