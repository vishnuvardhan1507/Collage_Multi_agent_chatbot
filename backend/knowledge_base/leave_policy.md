# Leave Policy

FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY: These are sample rules, not official college regulations.
For this demonstration, students may request up to 10 working days of ordinary leave
per semester. Medical or event exceptions require faculty review. This allowance does
not override the 75 percent attendance requirement. The assistant records requests;
faculty determine approval and semester allowance eligibility. The 10-working-day allowance
is subject to faculty review and is not automatically enforced by the assistant.

Students should submit leave requests before the absence whenever possible. A request must
include the from date, to date, and a clear reason. Dates must be ordered and cannot start
in the past. Requests cannot overlap the same student's pending or approved leave,
including a shared start or end date. Dates use the college timezone, Asia/Kolkata.

Chat submission displays a summary with Confirm and Cancel. No request is submitted until
Confirm is clicked and submission succeeds; Cancel creates no request. Typing a reply in
chat does not confirm submission. Proposals expire after 15 minutes, and a newer action
proposal in the same conversation replaces the previous pending proposal. The leave-panel
Submit button submits directly without the additional chat confirmation step.

Students may delete only their own requests whose end date is today or later, including
requests ending today. Requests that ended before today cannot be deleted. The leave panel
asks for Yes/No confirmation before deletion.

Medical leave longer than two working days should include supporting documentation when the
student returns to campus. Event participation leave should be routed through the faculty
coordinator or department office.

Faculty may review pending leave requests only for students in their own department.
Advisor or teaching assignments do not grant review access to another department's students.
Rejecting a request requires a reason, which is displayed to the student. Approved leave
may be considered when attendance shortage cases are reviewed, but it does not automatically
change attendance records or grant examination eligibility.
